 TORNADO PHYSICS - CHANGELOG
 v5.0.0.0 (The Real-Life Update) - changes since v4.0.0.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NEW FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[UI] Tornado Control Panel
- New in-game settings screen, opened with Right Ctrl + Right Shift + T
  (rebindable under Options > Controls as "Tornado Control Panel").
- Every console setting is now available from six tabs:
    Settings  - save to XML, auto-save on close, factory resets per category
    Physics   - radius, ejection power, heavy threshold, damage rates, lift
                bales/logs, indoor/outdoor damage, border safety, geo-fence,
                randomize, plus the real-life climate options
    Tuning    - suction, lift, chaos, hover height, max speed, core ratio,
                bale orbit/suction, ejection duration and interval
    Effects   - siren, danger ring, vehicle fires
    Modules   - destruction, recovery bill, map overlay, cargo spilling,
                livestock, road traffic
    Developer - telemetry HUD, position tracking, verbose log channels,
                status / hotspot / map overlay dumps, husbandry kill test
- Uses the game's own settings-page style, with tooltips on every option.
- Every change goes through the same handlers as the t_ console commands,
  so the panel and the console always agree.
- Destructive actions (resets, kill test) ask for confirmation first.

[UI] Translations
- The control panel, key name, alerts, map overlay and in-game messages
  are translated into English, German, French, Polish, Spanish, Italian
  and Dutch. Other game languages show English.
- In multiplayer every player reads server messages (animal losses, tow
  bills, outbreak warnings) in their own language and currency.

[GAMEPLAY] Wildlife Flees The Tornado
- Birds, deer, boar and other wild animals run or fly away from an
  approaching tornado; animals it catches vanish.
- The farm dog runs home to its doghouse when the storm gets close (it is
  never lost or harmed).
- Adjustable flee distance beyond the tornado radius (default 150 m).
- Works in multiplayer: each player's own wildlife reacts.
- Console: t_set wildlife [on|off|distance <m>|status], t_reset wildlife.
- Test tools: t_dev wildlife list / t_dev wildlife spawn <species> [distance]
  [count] spawns wildlife in front of you (the game's own spawn command
  only exists in the developer build).

[ECONOMY] Cost Scale
- Everything Tornado Physics charges (currently the emergency tow / recovery
  bill) now follows the savegame's economic difficulty, like the game's own
  running costs: Easy x0.4, Normal x0.7, Hard x1.0.
- New "Cost scale" setting (25% - 200%, default 100%) in the Modules tab
  for further tuning, e.g. with economy mods. Console: t_set money <percent>.

[GAMEPLAY] AI Traffic Roadblocks
- AI road traffic now stops before driving into a tornado, waiting like at a
  red light at the edge of a keep-out zone around the storm.
- The zone follows the tornado and is released when it moves on or despawns.
- Cars already inside the zone drive out instead of being trapped.
- Adjustable stop distance beyond the tornado radius (default 40 m).
- With the danger ring on, each roadblock is shown as a red post.
- Pedestrians stop at the edge of the zone too (both directions on every
  footpath), so nobody walks into the storm; people already inside walk out.
  Shown as yellow posts with the danger ring. FS25 gives mods no way to
  turn single pedestrians round, so they wait instead.
- Console: t_set traffic [on|off|buffer <m>|status], t_reset traffic.

[GAMEPLAY] Natural Hazards: Drought, Floods and Field Fires (optional, OFF by default)
- Long hot dry spells now dry out the soil. The mod tracks one soil-water level
  for the map from the game's own weather (rain fills it, warm dry hours drain
  it, below 5 C nothing dries out).
- Dry-spell warning when the soil gets low; a drought starts after two dry days.
  Every drought day each growing field loses a little more crop in patches (the
  game's own disaster damage, like hail), up to about 15 % with the default
  "mild" strength; rain ends the drought. Rice paddies are spared, grass is hit
  half as hard. Crop damage only with the game's disaster destruction setting.
- Owners get a daily "N of your fields lost crop" message; every hit is logged
  and saved for the coming crop insurance.
- Settings (Modules tab or t_set hazards): on/off, drought on/off, crop damage
  strength and dry-spell frequency (25 - 300 %). t_dev hazards forces a drought
  for testing. Switched off while FS25_RealisticWeather is installed.
- Floods: several hours of heavy rain on already soaked soil flood the lowest-
  lying growing fields of the map - water pools at each field's lowest point and
  drowns the crop there in patches. "FLOODING!" alert, damage messages to the
  owners, logged for the insurance. Rice paddies are spared.
- "Only damage owned fields" (default OFF): drought and floods leave fields no
  farm owns untouched.
- Field fires: during a drought, on hot dry afternoons (10:00 - 19:00, 25 C or
  more, no rain), a ripe grain-type field can catch fire. The fire starts at a
  spot inside the field and spreads as an uneven line of flickering flames,
  with a tall smoke plume that leans with the wind and can be seen from far
  away, burning the crop inside, until it burns out or rain puts it out. Red map marker (click to
  go there), alerts for start and end, burnt share logged for the insurance.
  One fire at a time; it stays inside its field. Seen by every player in
  multiplayer.

[ECONOMY] Insurance - groundwork (optional, OFF by default)
- First part of the coming per-farm insurance: on/off switch, who may manage
  policies (farm managers + admin, or the server admin only) and the insurance
  region (Auto detects the map's country and state / province; 14 countries
  and regions with real insurance data). Modules tab or t_set insurance.
- Monthly premiums and payouts get their own rows on the Finances page
  ("Insurance premiums", "Insurance payouts"); letters from the insurer appear
  as notifications. With Red Tape (by Ozz) installed, premiums count as a
  deductible expense and payouts as taxable income on its tax statement.
  Saved with the savegame, synced in multiplayer.
- Map crops: crops that maps add (rye, spelt, triticale, winter wheat,
  alfalfa, beans ...) are insured like their closest base-game crop; cover
  crops and green manure are not insurable. Crops that are unusual for the
  region (rice in Kansas, cotton in Germany) will cost extra.
- Storm damage report: after a tornado or hail storm, each farm gets one
  message per storm - how many of its fields were hit, about how many hectares
  of crop were lost and the worst field. Every crop loss (tornado, hail,
  drought, flood, field fire) is recorded per field for the coming crop
  insurance claims. Works with the insurance switched off too.
- Crop insurance for every supported country: insure all your farm's fields with
  one plan and a coverage level, Modules tab or console - US / Canada / Brazil
  yield and revenue plans, German and Polish hail / multi-peril packages, UK and
  Chilean named perils, Argentine hail covers, French and Dutch multi-risk,
  Spanish modules, Italian policy types and the Hutan Pantai rice scheme, each
  with its own subsidies, taxes and deductibles. Policies only pay for the
  perils they cover (a hail policy does not pay for a tornado). Australian
  and Canadian insurance are prepared but not available yet. Setting: sum insured from game
  values or real-world values. Real premium rates and subsidies,
  prices locked for the policy year, monthly premiums. Every recorded loss
  (tornado, hail, drought, flood, fire) opens a claim: an adjuster visits, a
  decision letter follows, then the payout. Losses within your uninsured part
  (e.g. 25 % at 75 % coverage) are not paid. Policies can only be bought in
  late winter / early spring (setting: auto / always / never).
- Equipment insurance: one policy covers every vehicle and tool your farm owns
  (not leased or mission vehicles). Choose the deductible ($1,000 / $2,500 /
  $5,000 / $10,000, x cost scale) - the higher it is, the lower the premium
  (1.5 % down to 0.8 % of the farm's vehicle value a year, billed monthly).
  The vehicle's value is recorded the moment the tornado reaches it, before any
  damage (Advanced Damage System's value when it is installed). When the storm
  is over a claim opens for every damaged vehicle: the estimate is the tow bill
  plus the engine and ADS repairs the storm caused. Repairs are paid minus the
  deductible. A vehicle that burned or whose repair would cost 75 % of its value
  is a total loss: accept (full value, the insurer takes the wreck) or buy it
  back (15 % less, you keep it with a Salvage title). No choice within a week =
  accepted. A Salvage title lowers the sell price by 50 %; once repaired it
  becomes Rebuilt, -30 % for good (both adjustable). Settings: replacement value
  (+15 %), remove or keep accepted total losses. Modules tab or
  t_dev insurance equip.
- Livestock insurance: one policy covers every animal of your farm against
  tornado losses at the animal's value when it dies (age and health count;
  replacement value setting +15 %). 0.8 % of the herd value a year, 5 % excess
  per claim. Poland uses its own data (subsidised rate, sum capped per head).
  On US maps the USDA Livestock Indemnity Program pays 75 % of the market
  value for free (no policy needed); a policy pays the rest. Animals only die
  with the Livestock module on. Modules tab or t_dev insurance livestock.

[UI] Insurance Tab
- New control panel tab for your farm's insurance. "Start here": choose crop
  (plan + coverage), equipment (deductible) and livestock cover, press Get
  quote to see what is insured, its value and the monthly price of each line
  (old -> new for a change) and the total, then Confirm & start buys / changes
  everything chosen at once. "My policies": every policy with its status,
  terms and price, and Cancel. Only farm managers and the admin can confirm or
  cancel; everybody can look at quotes. The buy rows left the Modules tab
  (it keeps the server-wide insurance settings).
- Policies grow with the farm: fields, vehicles and animals bought after the
  policy started are covered automatically, and the monthly premium is
  recalculated each month from what the farm owns. No need to buy again;
  the quote and My policies say so.

[UI] Messages Tab
- New control panel tab with the mod's messages to your farm (insurance
  letters, storm and damage reports, alerts, tornado warnings): recent messages
  stay for 24 in-game hours, save the ones you want to keep and remove them when
  you no longer need them.
- Full-page table: Recent / Saved lists with counts, a type filter (insurance,
  storm reports, hazards, tornado, recovery, other), Time / Type / Message
  columns, the selected message in full on the right with Save / Remove, and
  "Remove all saved". Mouse wheel, Up / Down buttons or a controller scroll it.
  A * marks messages that arrived since you last opened the tab, and the tab
  button shows how many.

[GAMEPLAY] Tornado Crop Damage Follows Its Size
- The strip of crop a tornado destroys now matches the tornado's real radius
  (the danger ring), the same in every direction and on every map - our own
  crop damage replaces the game's narrow one. Stronger tornadoes crush more of
  it. Heavy in the core, lighter toward the edge (crushed share core / middle
  / edge):
  EF-0 60 / 35 / 30 %, EF-1 75 / 50 / 45 %, EF-2 90 / 65 / 60 %,
  EF-3 95 / 75 / 70 %, EF-4 98 / 85 / 80 %, EF-5 98 / 90 / 85 %.
- Trees and destructible buildings: the game's tornado only ever broke them
  within 30 m. Now a bigger tornado reaches further (always inside 30 % of
  the radius, the game's 30 % tree chance up to 60 %).
- Bales are no longer deleted by the game's tornado (it silently removed
  every bale near the core); they are only thrown by our physics. Setting
  "Twister removes bales" (Physics tab, t_physics remove_bales) brings the
  base game's rule back.
- Both follow the savegame's "Disaster destruction" setting, like the game.

[COMPATIBILITY] Moisture System (FS25_MoistureSystem by Ozz)
- With Moisture System installed, our own drought switches off (Moisture System
  already withers crops in dry weather); field fires only start on fields it has
  dried out; floods keep working.
- Crops withered by Moisture System are recorded as drought damage for the crop
  insurance.

[COMPATIBILITY] Advanced Damage System 0.9.2
- A vehicle wrecked by the tornado now only gets real ADS breakdowns, not the
  condition states ADS manages itself (cold engine, general wear, engine jam ...).

[GAMEPLAY] Real-Life Tornado Climate (optional, OFF by default)
- When enabled, tornadoes follow real recorded statistics instead of the classic
  random roll. Turning it off keeps the classic behaviour exactly as before.
- Two countries:
    United States - NOAA Storm Prediction Center (1950-2025), US-wide + 50 states
    Canada        - Northern Tornadoes Project (2017-2026), Canada-wide +
                    Alberta, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Saskatchewan
                    (no Canadian hail records: hail borrowed from the
                    neighbouring US states MT, ND, MN, MI, NY)
  Smaller provinces use the Canada-wide data. More countries can be added
  as separate data files without changing the mod.
- Region: Auto or any region chosen manually. Auto recognises the map:
    1. a state, province or country named in the map title or mod name;
    2. otherwise the map's country - from a list of ~280 known ModHub maps,
       the base-game map, country / region words in the map description, or
       the base-game climate the map reuses - refined to a state / province
       named in the description (e.g. a US map "in the heart of Michigan");
    3. otherwise the classic roll (no guessing for unknown maps).
  "t_set climate status" shows the detected country and why.
- EF rating: rolled from the region's real EF mix (in the US about 56% EF0,
  32% EF1 ... 0.04% EF5). The tornado's size is placed inside its EF range
  using real track widths; the mod's own size and map scaling are kept.
- Storm weather: hail rolled from the region's real hail odds, with the stone
  size from the real size range (bigger stones = heavier hail fall). Wind
  gusts rolled from the region's real severe-wind data and applied as storm
  wind.
- Season and time of day: tornadoes come in the region's real tornado months
  and mostly in the late afternoon, while the map keeps its overall tornado
  rate. Adjustable "Tornado frequency" multiplier (0.25x - 4x).
  Southern-hemisphere maps are shifted by six months.
- Track direction: natural tornadoes travel the way they do in the region
  (in the US mostly toward the north-east), starting on the upwind side of
  the map. The console spawn still aims the way you are looking.
- Outbreak days: with the region's real odds, a touchdown can start a busy
  storm day. More tornadoes follow 30-120 in-game minutes apart, up to a
  configurable limit (default 3), until 22:00. All players get an outbreak
  alert.
- Each sub-feature (seasons, direction, outbreaks) can be toggled on its own.
- Console: t_set climate [on|off|region <name|code|auto>|list|status|
  seasons|direction|outbreaks|frequency <x>|outbreakmax <n>], t_reset climate.
- Climate data ships as small files, one per country (DB/TornadoClimate*.xml).
- Data sources and licences are credited in the mod description and README.

[UI] Map Overlay Additions
- Second line on the ESC map tornado overlay: storm type (LP / Classic / HP
  supercell), hail size and gusts (real-life mode), and the direction the
  tornado is moving (for example "Moving: NE").

[MULTIPLAYER] Full Multiplayer Support
- Clients now see exactly what the host sees: touchdown warning, siren, EF
  rating and radius, map hotspot, map overlay, compass marker, funnel size and
  extra debris, missing vehicle parts, smoke and fire, and the same storm
  weather (rain, hail, wind, direction).
- Animal-loss warnings reach every player; emergency tow bills go to the
  vehicle owner's farm.
- Players joining a running game receive the current settings, an active
  tornado, its weather and all damaged vehicles.
- Gameplay settings are shared and owned by the server. Only the host or a
  server admin can change them (same rule as the base game's settings); a
  change applies to every player. Non-admins see those options greyed out.
- Personal settings stay per player: siren sound, danger ring, map overlay,
  debug tools and auto-save. Joining a server never overwrites your own
  single-player configuration.

[SYSTEM] Tornado Saved With The Savegame
- A tornado that is on the ground when you save now comes back the same after
  loading: same EF rating, size, stats, storm weather and direction. No new
  roll, no second touchdown alert, no siren replay.
- A running outbreak carries on after loading instead of starting over.

[TOOLS] Test Twister Anywhere
- t_dev twister spawns a twister at your position right away, in any season.
  The game's gsWeatherTwisterSpawn fails when the map has no twister weather
  in the current season; this uses the map's summer twister then.
  Host / single player only.

[TOOLS] Climate Data Builder (modders only, not part of the mod download)
- tools/build_tornado_climate.py turns the NOAA SPC tornado, hail and wind
  CSV files into DB/TornadoClimate.xml. Re-run it when NOAA publishes a new
  year of data.
- tools/build_au_ca_climate.py does the same for the Australian (BoM) and
  Canadian (NTP) files: DB/TornadoClimate_AU.xml and DB/TornadoClimate_CA.xml.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BUG FIXES (existing issues from v4.0.0.0)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Errors / log messages
- Fixed "TORNADO DESTRUCTION SAVE ERROR: nil" printed on every save even when
  saving worked.
- Fixed a Lua error when Advanced Damage System total destruction ran with
  verbose logging on.
- Fixed Lua errors from log messages when verbose logging was enabled
  (for example running the husbandry kill test with t_dev verbose all).
- Fixed a possible error while re-linking saved destroyed vehicles that had
  no position or root node.
- Fixed a possible compass integration error with non-text marker ids.
- The startup log line now reads "Base Radius ... (before EF roll)" instead
  of looking like the tornado's actual radius.

Physics / gameplay
- Fixed the first tornado after loading a savegame using the base 35 m radius
  (and no map-size scaling) for suction, damage and traffic while the HUD
  showed the real size.
- Fixed t_tune purge_int having no effect (the ejection interval was only
  read once at map start).
- Fixed the tornado's debris particles multiplying again on every radius
  change or randomize (4x, 16x, 64x ...), which could hurt performance.
- Fixed smoke/fire fading and the 24-hour repair timer running at double speed
  while a tornado was active (several modules were updated twice per frame).
- Fixed the calendar jumping back when a tornado ended: the environment
  reload that repairs the twister weather also reset the date to day 6,
  08:00 and the days per month to 1 (often seen as "always August, 8 AM"
  after a storm). The date, time, days per month and fixed seasonal visuals
  are now kept. Check your days-per-month setting if you played with tornado
  ends before this fix.
- Fixed every vehicle's 24-hour auto-repair finishing the moment a tornado
  ended (the calendar jump above looked like a skipped day). The clock going
  back is no longer counted as skipped time; the smoke/fire time-skip check
  likewise.
- Fixed the funnel keeping the first roll's size when the tornado was
  re-rolled (t_physics randomize / radius, the control panel); the crop
  damage now follows every roll too.
- Fixed random fire mode making almost every damaged engine burn (the 50/50
  chance was re-rolled every frame, and again whenever the smoke effect was
  cleared - after a time skip or once the smoke faded). The roll is now made
  once per vehicle and kept until the vehicle is repaired.
- Vehicle fires are now rarer and depend on the tornado's strength: 5 % at
  EF0, 10 % EF1, 15 % EF2, 25 % EF3, 35 % EF4, 45 % EF5 for an engine past
  the ignite damage. The Effects tab switch is now "Vehicle fires": off means
  no vehicle fires at all (it used to mean every damaged engine burns).
- Fixed the 24-hour auto-repair countdown barely moving when time was
  fast-forwarded (EDC time skips, sleeping). Skipped time now counts fully.
- Fixed fractional chaos values in t_tune chaos being rounded away.
- Fixed cargo spilling from trailers still standing on their wheels (the
  lift height was measured from the terrain, so a high trailer frame or a
  farmyard / concrete surface counted as "in the air"). Lift is now
  measured from the trailer's own resting height and wheel ground contact.
- Fixed trailers keeping the last litres of cargo even when upside down
  (small amounts were too little for a heap and were put back every time).
- Cargo now spills at the same speed at any frame rate.
- Fixed covered trailers stopping to leak after the first few litres when
  standing on end or upside down (the trailer's own "no heap under me" area
  blocked every drop, and heaps were capped at 0.1 m).
- Restored wheel destruction: vehicles hit by the tornado can lose whole
  wheels (tire and rim) again, 0-2 per hit. Wheels are now taken from the
  game's own wheel list instead of part names, and are restored correctly
  after loading a savegame and by the 24-hour repair / recovery bill.
  Tracked vehicles keep their tracks: wheels inside a track, wheels without
  a tire and tracks that mods build as wheels are never removed.
- Tornadoes can now (rarely, 10% per hit) tear a whole track off a tracked
  vehicle. On the recovery bill a track costs 3% of the vehicle's value
  (at least $2,000, at most $40,000).
- Fixed vehicles being wrecked again (and billed twice when reset was pressed
  again) right after an emergency-tow reset during a tornado: FS25 loads the
  reset vehicle as a new copy and keeps the old one in the storm until it is
  ready. The old copy is now left alone while the reset is in progress.
- The recovery bill now prices lost wheels properly: 0.6% of the vehicle's
  value per wheel (at least $250, at most $8,000), instead of the $30 of a
  small body part.
- Fixed a removed decal or hose sometimes hiding a whole detail-level group
  ("lod0") of the vehicle model around it.
- The recovery bill now counts each removed part in the category it was
  removed as (trash / structural / wheel / track) instead of guessing from
  its name; the category is saved with the savegame.
- Fixed damaged vehicles showing the wrong missing parts after loading a
  savegame when several parts had the same name (e.g. two "glass" parts):
  hidden parts are now saved by their exact position in the vehicle.
  Older savegames still load.
- Fixed animal lethality checking the distance to the barn instead of the
  pasture: a tornado crossing the far side of a large pasture did nothing,
  while one passing the barn killed animals in a pasture it never touched.
  The pasture's real fence outline is used now.
- Fixed the pasture immunity never scaling with map size (it was always
  2 minutes). The setting is now the value for a 2 km map and grows with
  bigger maps (4 km = 2x, 8 km = 4x).
- Animal losses now update the barn's animal count and the visible animals
  right away.
- Fixed headers on a header trailer (and other dynamically mounted
  vehicles) staying locked to the trailer in the storm: they stood on end
  next to it and were pulled back onto it. Inside the wind field they are
  now released and fly as loose objects.
- Spawning a new tornado while one was already on the ground now gives it a
  new roll, touchdown alert, siren and storm weather (it was silently ignored).
- Fixed a reloaded savegame re-rolling the tornado that was already on the
  ground and showing its touchdown alert again.
- Fixed the shop-preview vehicle check never matching (it used a
  property state that does not exist in FS25).
- The tornado now only grabs map objects that have real physics. Objects the
  game holds in place (bales / pallets mounted on a trailer, growing trees,
  AI traffic, animated doors and gates) are no longer pushed around by the
  prop / log sweep; loose signs, cones and debris fly as before.

Weather
- Fixed tornado storm weather permanently changing the map's own rain and
  cloud settings: after one tornado, every later rain of that type kept the
  tornado's rain, hail and darkness for the rest of the session.

Settings / UI
- Fixed the danger-ring setting not being restored when loading a savegame.
- Fixed factory reset for recovery / destruction changing the wrong settings.
- Fixed the map hotspot disappearing for good after a tornado despawned if
  the environment could not be reloaded.
- The pasture debug message no longer fills the log every 5 seconds while
  animal lethality is on.

Recovery
- Resetting a tornado-damaged vehicle to the shop with the recovery bill now
  also restores its missing parts.

Multiplayer
- Fixed clients missing almost everything visual (siren, warnings, hotspot,
  overlay, funnel size, destroyed parts, smoke and fire); see Multiplayer
  Support above.
- Fixed every player getting different storm weather.
- Fixed a client changing the radius re-rolling the tornado on their own
  screen only.
- Fixed each player using their own local settings file instead of the
  server's.

Stability
- Game hooks (vehicle reset, weather, map screen, save) are no longer added
  again each time a savegame is loaded in the same game session.
